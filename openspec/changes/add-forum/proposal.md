# Proposal

## Why

The buttonweavers forum is where players organise tournaments, report bugs and chat, but it's no easier to read on a phone than the rest of the site. Reading it on button.men keeps players in one modern place.

## What Changes

Players can read the forum on button.men: boards, threads and posts. Posting and replying stay on buttonweavers for now.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `forum`: reading the buttonweavers forum on button.men
